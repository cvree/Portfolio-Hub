## Files no page currently serves

### Originals

Captures of the real products that the current layout does not place.
They are kept rather than deleted: each one is a capture of a running
build that cannot be re-taken from this repository, and each is evidence
behind a claim its case study makes in prose. Deleting them would cost
the record and save a few kilobytes that no visitor downloads.

- `assets/projects/health-journal/health-journal-patterns.webp`
- `assets/projects/phlebotomy/phlebotomy-question.webp`

### Unplaced derivatives

`tools/build_images.mjs` writes a 720 px and a 1280 px rendition of every
original, so a derivative of an unplaced original is unplaced too. They
are regenerated from the originals and are safe to remove at any time.

- `assets/projects/health-journal/health-journal-patterns-720.webp`
- `assets/projects/phlebotomy/phlebotomy-question-1280.webp`
