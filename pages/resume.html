Research

Alzheimer’s disease & dementia education

Collaborative student research · Health Science Research Methods, CSUCI

105 participants

  • Contributed to a team study measuring whether a short, structured educational intervention changes participants’ confidence around recognising, discussing and supporting Alzheimer’s disease and dementia.
  • Confidence was measured before and after the intervention across a sample of 105 participants; the post-intervention measure improved.
  • Reported at the size the design supports: a convenience sample without a control group demonstrates that a short intervention can move self-reported confidence, not a population-level effect.

Technical projects

Six independently designed, built and shipped products. Full case studies at cvree.github.io/Portfolio-Hub.

Health Journal

Local-first daily health record · React, Vite, installable PWA

Live

  • A private health journal that runs entirely on-device with no account and no server, and makes no network requests at all once loaded.
  • User-designed check-ins, a day timeline, sunlight sessions computed from real solar elevation, personal experiments and lab results with laboratory-supplied reference ranges preserved.
  • Findings are gated behind a four-rung evidence ladder measured in paired days; the app reports no confidence percentages, on the grounds that a percentage is a claim about a population.
  • Exports to CSV, multi-sheet Excel and JSON, plus a printable appointment pack ordered the way a consultation runs. Optional encrypted sync derives its key at 600,000 PBKDF2-SHA256 iterations, on-device.

Phlebotomy Exam Prep

NHA CPT certification study platform · Next.js, TypeScript, static export

Live

  • Authored 513 original certification-style questions across ten study areas and 181 original vocabulary definitions, with explanations that name why the specific distractor chosen was wrong.
  • Built four Order of Draw drill modes over the six CLSI collection positions and a four-direction tube-and-additive drill, each fully keyboard-operable.
  • Deterministic, explainable weak-area detection, per-area mastery and a study readiness score that states in plain language what it does and does not predict.
  • 257 automated tests, including structural assertions on the question bank so a content error fails CI rather than reaching a student.

OWCS Comp Tracker

Computer-vision evidence pipeline · Python, OpenCV, SQLite, static site

Live

  • Turns an esports broadcast VOD into an auditable hero-composition timeline: computational HUD calibration, gameplay-versus-replay frame classification, ranked template matching and a temporal swap model.
  • Detection returns UNKNOWN rather than a best guess; templates are promoted only after held-out validation, and hero readiness is reported as four separate measures rather than one number.
  • Human review gate where every detection is shown beside the frame it was read from, including the change points the model rejected and why. 107 offline Python test modules.

PaperAnimator

Research-presentation editor · React 19, TypeScript, WebCodecs, pdf.js

Live

  • Turns a research paper into a cited animated presentation entirely in the browser, with nothing uploaded and no account.
  • A marked passage carries the character-level quads of its source, so provenance is the selection itself and cannot be attached — or skipped — later. Every atom carries one of six provenance labels.
  • One pure render function drives the live canvas, the previews and the exporter, making preview-equals-export a type signature. Exports to MP4, PPTX, searchable PDF, PNG, SRT/VTT and timed transcript.

Manifester

Guided-audio session builder · React, Vite, PWA, Web Audio

Live

  • Loops a user’s own written lines over layered ambient sound with two device-independent studio voices, cached per line so a heard loop plays fully offline.
  • Background sound, voice, speed and pitch all change live under a running session without restarting speech, clock or breathing guide.
  • Exports a real MP3 rendered on-device in a Web Worker, with a WAV fallback and no upload.
