Research

Alzheimer’s disease & dementia education

Collaborative student research · Health Science Research Methods, CSUCI

105 participants

  • Contributed to a team study measuring whether a short, structured educational intervention changes participants’ confidence around recognising, discussing and supporting Alzheimer’s disease and dementia.
  • Confidence was measured before and after the intervention across a sample of 105 participants; the post-intervention measure improved.
  • Reported at the size the design supports: a convenience sample without a control group demonstrates that a short intervention can move self-reported confidence, not a population-level effect.
