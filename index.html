Research

Teaching people about a
disease they are afraid of.

Health Science Research Methods, CSU Channel Islands. I was one member of a student team, and the work is described here as it was done.

105

participants surveyed

The question was narrow: does a short, structured educational intervention change how confident people feel about Alzheimer’s and dementia — recognising it, talking about it, supporting somebody living with it?

Confidence was measured before and after across 105 participants, and the post measure improved. That is the whole finding: a convenience sample with no control group shows a short intervention can move self-reported confidence, not anything about a population.

Research Methods and Biostatistics are where I learned to state a result at the size it actually is — the discipline every project on this site needed.

The through-line

Five worlds,
one set of habits.

Emergency medicine, competitive esports, health research, club leadership and product design all reward the same small set of habits.

01

Decide with incomplete information

A patient assessment and a match review are the same exercise: gather what you can, act on the best reading available, and keep a record of why. Every project here shows its working.

02

Say only what the evidence supports

Health Journal refuses to report a finding until it has the paired days for it. PaperAnimator cannot write a sentence the paper does not contain. That restraint came out of research methods.

03

Design for the worst moment, not the demo

Blocked font hosts, dead networks, a phone in one hand. Every product here has a defined behaviour for the moment it stops going well — in the field, that is the moment that counts.

04

Lead by making the thing legible

Running a club is mostly reducing what people have to hold in their heads: one schedule, one place to ask, one obvious next step. The same instinct runs through every interface here.

05

Finish it

Six products with the edge cases handled, tests that fail on content mistakes, and privacy pages that render on a dead connection. Shipping is the part most projects skip.

06

Stay curious out loud

Sunlight and circadian rhythm, human performance, nutrition, the mechanics of a team fight. Most of this began as something I wanted to understand properly.
