Research

Teaching people about a
disease they are afraid of.

Health Science Research Methods at CSU Channel Islands. Collaborative academic research — I was one member of a student team, and the work is described here the way it was actually done.

105

participants surveyed

The project asked a narrow question: does a short, structured educational intervention change how confident people feel about Alzheimer’s disease and dementia — recognising it, talking about it, and supporting somebody living with it?

Confidence was measured before and after across a sample of 105 participants, and the post-intervention measure improved. That is the finding, and it is the whole of it: a student research project with a convenience sample and no control group is evidence that a short intervention can move self-reported confidence, not evidence about a population.

Being precise about the limits is part of the training. Health Science Research Methods and Biostatistics are where I learned to state a result at the size it actually is — which turned out to be the same discipline every project on this site needed.

The through-line

Five things that look
unrelated, and are not.

Emergency medicine, competitive esports, health research, club leadership and product design all reward the same small set of habits. This site is organised around that, not around a list of repositories.

01

Decide with incomplete information

A patient assessment and a match review are the same exercise: gather what you can, act on the best reading available, and keep a record of why. Every project here shows its working.

02

Say only what the evidence supports

Health Journal refuses to report a finding until it has the paired days for it. PaperAnimator cannot write a sentence the paper does not contain. That restraint came out of research methods.

03

Design for the worst moment, not the demo

Blocked font hosts, dead networks, a phone in one hand. Every one of these products has a defined behaviour for the moment it stops going well, because in the field that is the moment that counts.

04

Lead by making the thing legible

Running a club is mostly reducing what people have to hold in their heads: one schedule, one place to ask, one obvious next step. The same instinct runs through every interface here.

05

Finish it

Six products with real users’ worth of edge cases handled, tests that fail on content mistakes, and privacy pages that render on a dead connection. Shipping is the part most projects skip.

06

Stay curious out loud

Sunlight and circadian rhythm, human performance, nutrition, the mechanics of a team fight. Most of what is on this site began as something I wanted to understand properly.
