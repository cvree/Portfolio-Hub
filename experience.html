Research

Alzheimer’s & dementia
education.

Health Science Research Methods, CSU Channel Islands. Collaborative student research — I was one member of a team, and the description below is sized to what the study can actually support.

105

participants surveyed

The question. Does a short, structured educational intervention change how confident people feel about Alzheimer’s disease and dementia — recognising it, talking about it, and supporting somebody living with it?

The method. Confidence measured before and after the intervention, across a sample of 105 participants, as a collaborative project within a research methods course.

The finding. The post-intervention confidence measure improved.

What it does not show. A convenience sample with no control group is evidence that a short intervention can move self-reported confidence. It is not evidence about a population, and self-reported confidence is not the same thing as capability. Saying so is not modesty; it is the finding stated at its correct size.

This is the project that changed how I build software. Every product on this site now has a rule about what it is allowed to claim: Health Journal’s evidence ladder, PaperAnimator’s refusal to write a sentence the paper does not contain, OWCS Comp Tracker returning UNKNOWN rather than a best guess. All three are the same habit, learned here.
